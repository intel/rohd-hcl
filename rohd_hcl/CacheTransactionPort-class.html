CacheTransactionPort

CacheTransactionPort<T extends Logic, M extends Logic, C extends Logic, R extends Logic> class

Request, response, and eviction channels for a transactional cache port.

The caller provides requests and consumes responses and evictions. All three channels require stable offers until transfer. Reset is controlled by the enclosing implementation, not by this port.
