CsrSoftwareModel

CsrSoftwareModel class

Software-held state for an entire CSR top.

Addresses are the frontdoor's logical addresses (no implicit byte scaling). Inspection and loading bypass architectural write effects. Hardware frontdoor and backdoor writes retain their architectural semantics.
